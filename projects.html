Computational Fluid Dynamics

CFD Simulation of Flow Through an Orifice Plate

Computational fluid dynamics investigation of flow through an orifice plate using STAR-CCM+.

The project involved developing a CFD model, investigating mesh independence and convergence, and comparing the simulated pressure drop with theoretical predictions based on ISO 5167.

Mechanical Design

Actuated Sensor Alignment System for Subsea Applications

Engineering design of an actuated sensor alignment system for a gravity-moored subsea sensor platform.

The project investigates actuator configurations, materials, mechanical design and the effects of tidal forces on sensor alignment at depths of up to 100 m.
